# Memory

## Observed Patterns

### High Energy

* Exploring new ideas
* Connecting concepts across domains
* Talking with founders
* Understanding systems
* Product strategy
* AI workflows
* Teaching and explaining
* Discovering opportunities

### Low Energy

* Bureaucracy
* Fixed hierarchies
* Repetitive execution
* Micromanagement
* Politics
* Maintenance-heavy work

## Founder Fit Signals

Strong alignment with founders who:

* Think long-term
* Value creativity
* Like experimentation
* Are open to unconventional talent
* Care about impact, not just credentials

## Opportunity Signals

Interesting:

* Future of Work
* Creator Economy
* Education
* Wellness
* Human Potential
* AI-native businesses
